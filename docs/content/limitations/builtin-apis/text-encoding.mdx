- **Text encoding:** `TextEncoder` and `TextDecoder` can be stored in fields, arguments, return values, arrays, closures, and module-scope `var` bindings.
  Encoding supports UTF-8, including `encodeInto` into a Uint8Array or Buffer view.
  Codec properties expose the canonical encoding and decoder flags, and codecs print, format, and convert to strings and JSON as in Node.
  Decoding accepts recognized WHATWG labels, including runtime labels and omitted or undefined encodings.
  Constructor and decode options may be literals or runtime objects; `fatal`, `ignoreBOM`, and `stream` are read in Node's order.
  Streaming decodes support UTF-8, UTF-16, the single-byte encodings, GBK, gb18030, Big5, EUC-JP, Shift_JIS, and EUC-KR.
  A streaming ISO-2022-JP decode throws `SC2020` at run time, as does a streaming decode where a malformed multi-byte sequence starts in an earlier chunk, a case for which Node can throw `ERR_ENCODING_INVALID_ENCODED_DATA`.
  A `let` or `const` codec binding must be initialized at its declaration.
